Start here: the Faceless Video Checklist.

Five checks to run before you publish a product video. Free PDF.

You made a short product video. No face on camera, script drafted with AI help, visuals generated, music underneath. It feels done. It might not be.

This checklist covers the five things that decide whether that video is useful or just noise:

  1. The script check. Can you defend every sentence? Cut what you can't verify.
  2. Disclosure. Say the plain thing up front if you earn a commission.
  3. The link path. Click your own affiliate link before anyone else does.
  4. The Associate rules that bite beginners. Prices, ratings, account standing.
  5. The boring production pass. Audio, captions, and cutting the filler.

It comes from the Amazon Affiliate Videos Guide ($14.99). The checklist is the free version. The guide is the full workflow.

The honest bit This checklist won't get you views or sales by itself. It keeps you from publishing something sloppy. The work is still yours.
