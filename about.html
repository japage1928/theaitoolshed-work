About

I'm John. I'm an over-the-road truck driver.

I make about $1,450 a week after taxes hauling freight. I've been driving for around six years. The money covers the bills and not much else, so I'm building remote income on the side with the goal of getting off the road for good.

I build these guides in short windows between runs, usually from the cab or the kitchen table at home. If a guide says a task takes an hour, it's because it took me an hour.

I write the way I wish someone had written for me when I started: plain words, real steps, no promises. Every guide on this site says what's inside, how long it is, what it costs, and what it won't do. If I haven't tested something myself, I say so.

I'm not a guru. I'm a guy with a laptop trying to build a second income without quitting the first one yet. If that's your situation too, you're in the right place.
